WiFi Access Analyzer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1

Information We Collect

This app collects the following data to provide its core features:

  • Location data – Required by Android to scan nearby WiFi networks. GPS is never activated or stored.
  • WiFi network information – Network names (SSID), signal strength, and channel data are scanned and displayed in real-time.
  • App preferences – Settings such as auto-scan and notification preferences are saved locally on your device.
2

How We Use Your Data

  • Location permission is used solely for WiFi scanning as required by Android.
  • WiFi scan results are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.
  • App preferences are stored on-device using Android SharedPreferences.
3

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any user data to third parties.

4

Permissions

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATIONCOARSE_LOCATION – Required by Android to scan WiFi networks.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATECHANGE_WIFI_STATE – Used to read WiFi signal and network details.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – Used to detect connection changes for network alerts.
You can revoke permissions anytime via Android Settings → Apps → WiFi Access Analyzer → Permissions
5

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date will reflect any changes.
